# Third-party components

## Female shoulder-line bust 

**Scifi Girl v.01** by **patrix**.
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/scifi-girl-v01-96340701c2ed4d37851c7d9109eee9c0
License: **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International**.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The author's page describes hand-created and painted textures. This is an authored
character, not a photographed scan. The user supplied the original OBJ archive.
Only the head, neck and shoulders remain, with open eyes and separate lashes.
Body geometry, clothing, accessories, fur and original hair cards are excluded.
Unused full-body areas of the skin textures are replaced with neutral pixels.
The retained geometry is refined, normalized and adapted for cervical motion,
lighting and the existing hairstyle comparison. Gloss is converted to roughness;
the normal-map convention is adapted.

The adapted female GLB and female previews without the separate BY-SA hair retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
they are not MIT assets. Redistribution requires attribution, a license link,
an indication of adaptations, noncommercial use and the same license for the
adaptation. Credit remains here, in the bundled notice, the GLB copyright field
and exported JPEG metadata, without a visible source stamp in the studio.

Blender is a local authoring tool and is not bundled with the app.

## Male shoulder-line bust and beard 

**Man base mesh** by **BlakkMato**. The inspected original listing marks the
asset **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/man-base-mesh-a870a28384fb428da535b6a816fea73e
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The user supplied the original OBJ archive and textures. Adaptations: retain
only head, neck and shoulders; remove arms, torso and source hair/beard cards;
retain the eyes, separate eye materials, refine the surface, mask unused body
regions in textures, adapt normal/specular maps, and normalize for cervical motion.
A new 22,000-strand beard groom replaces the wide alpha cards. It includes
separate growth patterns for moustache, cheek hair, chin hair and stubble;
follicle-based skinning keeps tips attached to the face during neck motion.
`dist/assets/male-portrait.glb`, `male-beard.bin` and male previews without the separate hair retain CC BY 4.0 as adaptations. Male previews with BY-SA hair also carry the hair terms; they are not solely BY 4.0. Credits remain in this file, the bundled notice,
the GLB copyright and exported JPEG metadata. The normal studio UI is unmarked.
This is a textured authored model; do not label it a photographic head scan.
